ESG Award 2027

Bars — Guardian of the Future

The award evaluates verified results, not pledges: 14 categories and a Grand Prix, an independent jury, open criteria and a public finalist defence.

1 March 2027 — 30 June 2027

Apply

Principles

  • Independent assessment
  • Open criteria
  • Verifiable data
  • The award cannot be bought

14 categories by block

Grand Prix “ESG Leader of the Year” — Individual award; self-nomination is not allowed.

Environment

  • Leader in Decarbonization

    Companies

    Verified emissions reduction and plan to the target year

  • Responsible water and nature stewardship

    Companies, public and utility organisations

    Water use reduction, ecosystem restoration, biodiversity protection

  • Circular economy project

    Companies, cities, NGOs

    Recycling/reuse volume and economic sustainability of the model

Social

  • Social partnership of the year

    Joint company + NGO/social centre application

    Outcomes for beneficiaries, partner equality, duration of collaboration

  • Employer for people

    Companies

    Occupational safety, inclusion, learning, conditions for employees and communities

  • NGO or social centre of the year

    NGOs, social centres, social enterprises

    Measured social impact, transparency, organisational resilience

Governance

  • Best ESG report and transparency

    Companies and financial organisations

    Completeness and comparability of disclosure, standards alignment, assurance

  • Board of the year

    Companies with an active board

    Board role in ESG strategy, independence, diversity, risk oversight

  • Sustainable finance deal of the year

    Issuers, banks, deal arrangers

    Deal structure, use of proceeds, independent verification

  • ESG team of the year

    Sustainability teams of companies and financial organisations

    Team results, influence on leadership decisions, cross-team engagement

Next Generation

  • Youth initiative of the year

    Teams/organisations led by someone under 30

    Results of an operating project and peer engagement

  • University of the year

    Higher education institutions

    ESG in curricula, research and campus management

  • ESG champion among SMEs

    Small and medium-sized enterprises

    Practices proportionate to business scale and their impact

  • Best AI solution for ESG

    Developors and adopters; joint applications allowed

    Measured adoption impact, data quality, responsible AI use

Criteria and scores

  • 30 · Measurable result

    Actual indicators versus baseline

  • 20 · Scale and significance

    How many people, resources or territories are affected

  • 15 · Systems and governance

    Embedded in strategy, owners, budget and controls

  • 15 · Novelty of approach

    What is done differently from industry or regional practice

  • 10 · Replicability

    Whether the practice can be repeated elsewhere in the region

  • 10 · Data transparency

    Evidence quality, verification, public disclosure

Finalist score = 70% application + 30% public defence. No award below 60 points.
