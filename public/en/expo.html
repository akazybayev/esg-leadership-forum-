Zones

  • Guest-country pavilion (5 sections)

    A single block at the ESG Expo entrance for guest-country companies and organisations — in addition to 20 exhibitor stands. Country announced after package confirmation.

  • Technologies and solutions

    Clean energy and climate; water, resources and circular economy; ESG data, digitalisation and AI.

  • Partnerships

    Business, university and NGO projects; youth initiatives; business meetings in the foyer.

  • Reusable stands

    Modular builds, reuse after the forum, unified ESG Leadership Forum style.

  • Youth and social projects (zone 4)

    Dedicated zone on the floor plan next to the coffee break.

  • Key partner stands (zone 5)

    Partner visibility in the central foyer.

Exhibitor conditions

  • Up to 20 exhibitors plus 5 guest-country pavilion sections; exact booth layout agreed with Hilton Astana.
  • Registration, photo zone and coffee break remain. Exact booth layout is agreed with Hilton Astana.
  • Guest access to the foyer exhibition is free.
  • NGOs and social projects receive delegate promo quotas.
