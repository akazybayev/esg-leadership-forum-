Guest country of the forum

A separate format of international partnership

A status for one country: a country session on the main stage, a national pavilion of 5 sections, an on-site B2B office, a welcome by the head of delegation at the opening, and a national evening. Terms are discussed individually with an embassy or an authorised agency.

The buyer is typically an embassy, a trade and investment promotion agency or a line ministry.
