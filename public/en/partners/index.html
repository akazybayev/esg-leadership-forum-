For partners

Become a partner of ESG Leadership Forum 2027

On 13–14 October 2027 Astana brings together 500 leaders of companies, banks, public and international organisations, universities and NGOs from 20+ countries. Partnership is not a logo on a banner. It is a role in the programme: a session you lead, an award you present, and the people you meet.

What every partner receives

  • Logo on the website, in the programme, on stage and in forum materials, with the partnership status
  • Delegate seats with access to every session, lunches, the reception and the gala dinner
  • Access to the B2B Lounge and scheduled one-to-one meetings
  • A mention in press releases and the forum’s final document
  • The participant list after the forum, with their consent

Packages

Guest country of the forum

by agreement

1 country. Terms are discussed individually with an embassy or an authorised agency.

  • • A country session on the main stage
  • • A national pavilion of 5 sections
  • • An on-site B2B office
  • • A welcome by the head of delegation at the opening
  • • A national evening

General partner

75,000 USD

1 company, whose name stands next to the name of the forum.

  • • “General partner” status in all materials, and a first-level logo
  • • Welcome remarks at the opening ceremony
  • • A keynote or a seat on the main plenary panel
  • • ESG Award gala partner: presenting the “ESG Leader of the Year” grand prize with the jury chair
  • • The premier ESG Expo stand — 4 sections at the entrance
  • • 20 delegate seats and two tables at the gala dinner
  • • A closed meeting of the company’s leadership with speakers and international guests
  • • Branding of the Main Stage and participant badges
  • • A studio interview and a dedicated press release on the partnership
  • • First choice of a package for the 2028 forum

Strategic partner

30,000 USD

3–4 companies that shape the forum agenda with the organiser.

  • • “Strategic partner” status and a second-level logo
  • • A speaking seat on a plenary panel or a keynote, agreed with the programme committee
  • • An ESG Expo stand — 2 sections
  • • 10 delegate seats and a table at the gala dinner
  • • Priority scheduling of one-to-one meetings in the B2B Lounge
  • • Branding of one zone: Hall B, the B2B Lounge or the coffee-break area
  • • A seat for a representative on the forum’s programme committee

Theme-track partner

10,000 USD

5 companies. Five tracks — Lead, Finance, Planet, People, Region — each with its own partner.

  • • Co-organising the track: agreeing session topics and speakers with the programme committee
  • • Moderator or speaker in a track session
  • • Hall branding during the track sessions
  • • An ESG Expo stand — 1 section
  • • 5 delegate seats
  • • Logo on the track page and in the programme

ESG Award category partner

10,000 USD

Up to 14 categories across Environment, Social, Governance and Next Generation.

  • • The partner’s representative presents the award at the gala
  • • Logo in the category name on the website, at the ceremony and on the certificates
  • • 5 delegate seats and places at the gala dinner
  • • A mention in the winners’ press release
  • • A category partner does not enter that category, and the partnership does not affect the jury

Youth programme patron

10,000 USD

1–2 partners who fund the participation of young leaders.

  • • Named scholarships for youth delegates aged 18–30, carrying the partner’s name
  • • A seat on the Youth Impact Pitch final jury
  • • Presenting the grant to the winner at the forum close
  • • The partner’s representative mentors one finalist team
  • • 5 delegate seats
  • • Logo on all youth-programme materials and in the ESG Youth Ambassadors network

NGO alley partner

25,000 USD

1 partner, thanks to whom NGOs and social centres exhibit free of charge.

  • • An NGO alley in the partner’s name: 10–15 mini-stands that NGOs and social centres receive free of charge
  • • Opening the business-and-NGO partnership fair
  • • A seat on the day-two plenary about business and NGO partnership
  • • 15 delegate seats for the partner and NGO representatives from the alley
  • • Logo on the alley and in the catalogue of social projects

ESG Expo exhibitor

5,000 USD per section

20 stands for companies, solutions and technologies.

  • • An equipped exhibition section for both days; two or more sections can be taken
  • • 2 delegate seats
  • • An exhibitor card on the website and in the exhibition catalogue
  • • Coffee breaks are held in the exhibition zone, so the flow of participants to the stand is guaranteed
  • • A chance to present on the Impact Stage (15 minutes, by agreement)

Prices are in US dollars, excluding VAT.

Partners without a fee

International organisations, embassies, line ministries, business associations and media take part as institutional and information partners: they bring the forum speakers, status and reach.
