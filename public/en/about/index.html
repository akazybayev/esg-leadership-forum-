Address by the forum initiator

Dear colleagues, partners and friends,

I invite you to ESG Leadership Forum 2027 – a forum we designed as a place for people who connect sustainable development with investment, management and business growth. The theme is “Leadership for Generations”. It is not a slogan. It is a commitment: the decisions we take today decide the country and the planet our children will live in.

In recent years ESG in Kazakhstan and Central Asia has moved from a fashionable term to a working tool. Banks issue green bonds, companies publish non-financial reports, the state is building a carbon-regulation system, and universities are opening sustainability programmes. I have seen this path from the inside – creating the National ESG Club, developing green finance at the AIFC, and training managers at ESG Management School and on the EMBA. What is missing is not ideas, but a place where ideas become agreements and projects.

That is why the forum is built around decisions, not speeches. Five tracks – Lead, Finance, Planet, People, Region – cover corporate governance and ESG ratings, sustainable finance and carbon markets, the energy transition and the circular economy, human capital and business partnerships with NGOs, and joint projects of Central Asia and partner countries. Each track ends not with a resolution, but with commitments we will check a year later.

The forum brings together four formats: a business programme with company leaders, investors and representatives of public and international organisations; ESG Expo, where technology meets buyers; the independent ESG Award with 14 nominations and a grand prix; and a youth programme – 100 delegates and the Youth Impact Pitch final. Leadership for generations means young leaders sit in the same room as the people who decide today.

On 13–14 October 2027 we expect 500 participants from more than 20 countries in Astana. Come with a case, a question or a project – and leave with partners.

See you in Astana.

Aidar Kazybayev

Initiator of the ESG Leadership Forum

Founder of Global ESG Partnership and ESG Management School

Organiser

Global ESG Partnership

An expert consortium in sustainable development, ESG and green finance. Astana, since 2023.

Global ESG Partnership is the organiser of ESG Leadership Forum Astana 2027.

In three years the partnership has delivered more than 50 consulting projects. The consortium brings together 30+ consultants, partners and analysts, with clients in 10+ countries.

The partnership includes ESG Management School, Central Asian Rating Agency, Art Ecology, Ashra Solutions, 2Wings, Green Investment Group, the Centre for Environmental Competences (cek.kz) and the International Institute of Management and Audit. Its mission is to inspire innovation and sustainable development. At the forum the partnership runs the business programme, the exhibition, the independent ESG Award and the youth programme. A partner chooses a role in the content, participation is set by agreement, and a report with metrics follows the forum.

Aidar Kazybayev

Founder and chairman of the board of Global ESG Partnership, initiator of the forum. Candidate of Economic Sciences.

More than 25 years in finance, public administration and sustainable development. A certified independent director, an accredited environmental auditor and an expert in greenhouse-gas validation and verification (ISO 14066).

He served on the Expo 2017 bid committee and worked on the theme “Future Energy”. In 2020–2023 he headed the AIFC Green Finance Centre, and in 2017–2020 he was managing director of the AIFC. Earlier he was director of the international relations department of the National Bank, chair of the trade committee, chair of the small-business committee and chief of staff to the minister of industry and trade.

He took part in the first green, social and gender bond issues in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan and in the first green token in Central Asia. His projects include the gasification of the Almaty combined heat and power plant and the Kulanak hydropower investment. He founded the National ESG Club and the Kazakhstan Biodiversity Fund, together with UNDP. He launched Kazakhstan’s first EMBA programmes in corporate governance, digitalisation and ESG. Since 2025 he has been president of the International Institute of Management and Audit. He is the author of “Future Energy”.

ESG Management School

An international school of ESG management. Education partner of the forum and the youth programme.

The school trains leaders to manage sustainable development. Together with L. N. Gumilyov Eurasian National University it runs the EMBA “ESG and Sustainable Development”, the first programme of its kind in Kazakhstan, and courses for boards and management on corporate governance and ESG reporting.

At the forum the school runs the student masterclass “A Career in ESG”, the rectors’ round table “University Alliance” and issues masterclass certificates.
