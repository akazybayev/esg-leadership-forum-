How do I get a promo code?

Free delegate places by promo code go to speakers, moderators and the jury, youth delegates, NGOs on the social-projects alley, ESG Award finalists (two places), accredited media and sponsors within package quotas. Request a code: forum@esgpartners.kz.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a visa?

Citizens of a number of countries can enter Kazakhstan visa-free for up to 30 days. Delegates who need a visa can request an invitation. Write to forum@esgpartners.kz.

Can I pay by card?

Payment is currently by invoice. Online card payment (Visa/Mastercard, Kaspi) will be added later — registered participants will be notified.
